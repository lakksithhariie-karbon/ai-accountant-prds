Product Requirements Document

Bill Splitter

Multi-invoice PDF boundary detection and guarded handoff to AI Accountant

The splitter owns boundaries; AIA owns voucher fields. One supplier packet goes in. The system proposes bill boundaries, the human confirms them, and each clean invoice PDF lands in AI Accountant Needs Review.

The Problem#

What is happening today?

AI Accountant (AIA) is designed to extract a single bill from a single PDF. When a user uploads a multi-invoice PDF, such as a 30-page supplier packet containing 20 invoices, AIA treats the whole file as one bill and runs it through its extraction flow.

That flow has a hard reading limit of 12 pages per bill: max_downstream_pages = 12. A 30-page packet exceeds it and is rejected with a message asking the user to split the file into smaller PDFs and upload again.

Even when the packet is small enough to be read, AIA still tries to extract one voucher from many invoices. Vendors, amounts, and pages can land on the wrong bill, creating a mixed-up voucher.

Input reality

One PDF can contain many independent invoices, plus annexures, signatures, terms, covers, and blank separators.

AIA expectation

One PDF must represent one bill, and that bill must stay under the downstream 12-page reading cap.

Who is affected?

Accounts-payable users who receive supplier packets with multiple invoices bundled into a single PDF. This is common for vendors that batch bills into monthly statements or attach annexures and signature pages to each bill.

Why is it a problem?

The user's real input does not match what AIA can ingest. Users must manually split packets into individual PDFs before uploading. That work is repetitive, slow, and error-prone. If they skip it, the upload is either rejected or produces a voucher that somebody must untangle later.

Impact if we do not solve it

  • Rejected files and repeated manual PDF splitting.
  • Wrong bills when vendors, amounts, and pages are mixed into one voucher.
  • Reviewer rework to identify and repair extraction mistakes.
  • Reduced trust in automated AP intake.

Job Stories / User Stories#

Who is the user?

An accounts-payable operator who receives multi-invoice PDF packets and needs each invoice to reach AI Accountant as a clean, correctly named single-bill file.

What are they trying to do?

Turn one multi-bill PDF into separate, correctly named bills that AIA can extract, without manually cutting the PDF.

Context

They have a packet PDF, often 20 or more pages, sometimes with annexures, terms, and signature pages, and need every bill to reach AIA's Needs Review queue.

Desired outcome

Every bill lands in AIA as its own single-invoice PDF, under the page limit, named so it is identifiable, with no manual splitting and no mixed-up vouchers.

Job story 01. When I upload a multi-invoice PDF, I want the system to read every page and mark where each bill starts, so that I do not have to split the file by hand.

Job story 02. When I review the proposed bill boundaries, I want to confirm or correct each cut and exclude cover or junk pages, so that only real invoices are pushed and nothing is dropped or merged incorrectly.

Job story 03. When I push the confirmed bills, I want each one to land in AI Accountant as a separate, correctly named PDF under the page limit, so that AIA can extract it cleanly and I can find it in Needs Review.

Scope#

In scope

  • Upload a multi-invoice PDF and create a review session.
  • Three-layer boundary detection: deterministic signals, Mistral OCR plus a boundary judge, and a post-judge overlay.
  • A review surface where the user confirms, corrects, and excludes boundaries, and edits vendor / invoice-number slots.
  • A flag model that surfaces uncertainty as start-class Looks right and hold-class Split here actions.
  • Filename planning in the packet-VEN3-number.pdf format, previewed in the UI and mirrored by the backend.
  • A guarded push to AI Accountant, Mode A, that lands each confirmed bill in Needs Review, never Approve.

Out of scope

  • Field extraction inside this tool. Extraction is only a demo or debugging instrument. AIA owns voucher fields.
  • A local history UI for past sessions. Sessions live on an ephemeral filesystem and reset on redeploy.
  • Production AIA writes unless an operator explicitly sets both AIA_API_BASE and AIA_ALLOW_WRITES=true.
  • Mode B, creating a bill without a PDF, or any Approve path.
  • Any call to POST …/tally/bill.

User Flow#

01

Enter

The user opens Bill Upload and chooses a PDF. Upload is capped at MAX_UPLOAD_PAGES, default 100. Larger files are rejected with a clear message.

02

Upload

The backend validates the PDF, records page count and dimensions, and creates a session. The app navigates directly to split review and starts detection immediately. There is no separate Detect button.

03

Detect

The stream head shows a loader and typewriter copy while the three-layer detector runs. The inspector says “Working on N pages” and “Finding where each bill starts.” No bills are shown yet.

04

Review

Once detection finishes, the stream shows proposed cuts and the inspector lists derived bills. The user can toggle boundaries, exclude pages, edit vendor and invoice numbers, bulk rename, acknowledge uncertain starts, and resolve held dividers.

05

Push

The user clicks “Push N invoices to AI Accountant.” Every confirmed bill is cut into its own PDF, named packet-VEN3-number.pdf, and pushed via the four-step DMS handshake.

06

Success end state

Each bill sits in AIA Needs Review as a clean, correctly named single-invoice PDF under the page limit. Review and Approve happen in AIA, not here.

Solution#

High-level approach. The splitter owns boundaries; AIA owns voucher fields. The system proposes where each bill starts, the human confirms and corrects the cuts, and each confirmed bill is pushed to AIA as a separate, correctly named PDF.

Upload

POST /api/upload validates the PDF and returns session id, page count, thumbnails, and per-page dimensions. Uploads are capped to bound OCR cost.

Three-layer detection

Layer 1 · deterministic signals
boundary_constraints.py

High-precision, partial-recall rules that the judge must not violate.

  • MUST SPLIT on page-dimension change, “Page 1 of N”, different own document numbers, different seller GSTINs, or a closing totals block followed by a real document start.
  • MUST NOT SPLIT on a shared document number, continuation marker, declared extent, or closing page.
  • Conflicts resolve by signal rank. A wrong hard split is treated as worse than a missed one.
Layer 2 · Mistral OCR + boundary judge
mistral.py

OCR reads every page. An LLM compares adjacent pages and marks likely invoice starts, seeded by the heuristic and constrained by Layer 1. Large packets are judged in overlapping windows so no page is dropped. On provider failure, the system falls back to the heuristic and returns an error code.

Layer 3 · post-judge overlay
boundary_overlay.py

For adjacent pairs sharing a grounded document number, the overlay merges when vendors match, blocks when vendors differ, and suggests when a letterhead is unreadable. Blank separator pages are auto-excluded.

Flag model

doc-needs-look.ts converts review uncertainty into two action classes.

Start-class

Reasons such as “this start”, “same bill”, and “letterhead weak” offer a Looks right acknowledgement. It is session-local and disappears if the start cut disappears.

Hold-class

Reasons such as “held” and “after-check merged” mark a conflict at a held divider. The user must explicitly split there before the flag clears.

Review surface

SplitReviewScreen.tsx and BoundaryStream.tsx provide a page stream with boundary toggles and exclusions, plus an inspector listing derived bills with editable vendor and invoice-number slots, packet prefix, bulk rename, and per-bill status.

Filename planning

aiaFilenames.ts and filenames.py produce packet-VEN3-number.pdf. VEN3 is the first three characters of the sanitized vendor, uppercased, or NA when empty.

  • Vendor and invoice number must be grounded on the document's pages or they are discarded.
  • User edits are authoritative.
  • Collision suffixes -2, -3, and so on apply in document order.
  • Naming never blocks push. Positional names are the fallback.

Guarded AIA push

client.py and push.py implement Mode A, one invoice PDF per file.

  1. upload-file-to-dms returns UUID plus presigned URL.
  2. upload-file sends the base64 body.
  3. PATCH status to uploaded with triggerEvent.
  4. Register through bill-status, then poll to a terminal HITL result.

Push is concurrent, default 4, dry-run by default, and gated. Live writes require both AIA_API_BASE and AIA_ALLOW_WRITES=true.

Key Rules & Logic#

  • Coverage invariant: documents ∪ excluded pages must cover pages 1..N exactly.
  • A document means one bill's worth of paper. Annexures and signature pages stay with the bill.
  • Same-supplier non-invoice pages may glue into one cut. Those are excluded before push, so the cost is an extra click, not a wrong voucher.
  • Same-supplier invoices with different bill numbers must stay separate.
  • Duplicate-number, different-vendor pairs must stay separate.
  • Never Approve.
  • Never POST …/tally/bill.

Important States & Edge Cases#

AnalyzingNo bills rendered. The stream head shows a loader and typewriter copy. The inspector shows “Working on N pages”.
After analyzeMeta line shows “N pages · AI found M invoices · K excluded”.
Detection failureShow a “Boundary detection failed” alert with the reason. Re-run detection is available.
Oversized billIf a derived bill exceeds 12 pages, skip it with the AIA page-limit message. Do not push it.
Duplicate invoiceIf the supplier invoice number already exists in AIA, skip as duplicate.

Stuck Bill Uploads rowsAfter recovery attempts, abort new launches to avoid flooding the tenant.

Relevant UX & Product Requirements#

  • The human stays in control. AI proposes boundaries, deterministic rules constrain them, and the user resolves uncertainty before anything is pushed.
  • Writes stay gated so demo traffic can never write into a customer ledger.
  • The UI shows exactly what filename will land in AIA before the user pushes.
  • The product path ends at a pushed invoice in AIA Needs Review. Extraction and approval happen in AIA.

Non-negotiable product boundary. This tool prepares clean invoice PDFs for AIA. It does not become another invoice-processing application.
